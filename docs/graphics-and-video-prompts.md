# 5280 Content — Hero & Icon Graphic Prompts

Ready-to-paste prompts for a designer or an AI image tool (Midjourney, DALL·E, etc.) to jumpstart hero images and icon sets for each product. All prompts assume the existing brand kit: Deep Navy `#0B1D2D`, Slate Blue `#334155`, Steel Blue `#64748B`, Light Gray `#E2E8F0`, Orange `#F97316`, White, set in Montserrat.

The matching AI video-short prompts are also included at the bottom, and now live in the repo itself under `assets/video-prompts/` so they travel with the codebase.

## Chairside — SMS booking & reminders for stylists/salons

**Hero (16:9 banner):**
> A modern, minimalist flat-illustration hero graphic for a salon-booking SMS app. Show a stylized salon chair in profile with a smartphone floating beside it displaying a text-message bubble with a small calendar/clock icon. Clean geometric shapes, soft rounded corners, generous negative space. Color palette: deep navy (#0B1D2D) background or accents, warm orange (#F97316) as the single highlight color, light gray (#E2E8F0) and white for secondary shapes. No photorealism, no gradients beyond a subtle two-tone shadow, no text baked into the image. Leave empty space on one side for a headline to overlay.

**Icons (flat line-art, orange stroke, transparent background, 64x64):**
- Text-message bubble with a small clock inside
- Calendar with a checkmark
- Chat/reminder bell

## Massive Empire Fantasy Football — original sci-fi command-room aesthetic

This one should feel inspired by space-opera command bridges without copying any specific franchise's logos, ships, uniforms, or insignia — an original "tactical hologram" visual language.

**Hero (16:9 banner):**
> A cinematic, dark sci-fi command-bridge illustration for a fantasy football strategy tool. A holographic tactical display floats above a dark control console, showing glowing football-field-shaped data grids and player markers instead of star charts. Deep navy and black background, glowing orange (#F97316) and cool blue holographic light as the only bright colors. Original sci-fi command-center aesthetic — no recognizable logos, insignias, uniforms, or ship designs from any existing film franchise. Wide banner, dramatic lighting, empty space on one side for headline text, no text baked into the image.

**Icon style note (apply to all 12 below):** flat, single-color line-art (orange or light gray on a dark navy/transparent background), minimal holographic-console feel, no text, no franchise trademarks, consistent stroke weight, square canvas.

**Draft-Season Tools:**
- Holotable Mock Draft Simulator — a holographic table/grid emitting upward light rays
- Imperial Draft Command — a control console with a directional arrow/target reticle
- Live Draft Surveillance — an eye or radar-sweep inside a circular scope
- Market Value Index — an ascending bar chart with a small currency/credits glyph
- Casualty Forecast — a shield icon with a small crack or warning pulse
- Bloodline Report — a lineage tree rendered as glowing geometric circuit nodes

**In-Season Tools:**
- Waiver Wire Bounty Board — a crosshair/target with a small tag icon
- Trade Tribunal — a balance/scale rendered as glowing geometric lines
- Command Bridge — a captain's-chair/console silhouette viewed from above
- Deployment Orders — a checklist/manifest with a directional arrow
- League Surveillance Grid — a grid of small radar blips
- Garrison Value Ledger — a stacked-ledger/tablet with glowing data lines

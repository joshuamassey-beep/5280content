Privacy policy

This policy is for 5280 Content LLC in Denver, Colorado, and it covers this website only.

Who we are

5280 Content LLC ("we") runs this site. We are in Denver, CO. For a privacy question, email hello@5280content.com.

What the contact form collects

The home page and some product pages have a "Get in touch" form. If you send it, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • Which page opened the form, so the email subject can be labeled from a fixed list

The form also sends a hidden field that people are not asked to fill in. If that field has anything in it, we treat the submission as spam and do not email it.

The form is sent to this site at /api/contact. We email it to ourselves through Resend so we can reply. Your email address is the reply-to address on that message. We do not save contact-form submissions in a database on this website.

If the email cannot be sent, the host's server log can include the name, email address, and page name from the form.

What the website brief collects

The page at /web-design/brief asks what a business wants in a site. If you send it, we collect the business name, your name, and email address, plus anything else you choose to fill in: phone, current website, whether you own a domain and the domain name, industry, service area, which add-ons you want, other requests, a launch timeframe, whether you have photos and text, and an optional budget range. The basic package is included on every brief.

The form is sent to /api/brief. We email that brief to 5280 Content through Resend. We use it only to scope and quote the project. We do not save briefs in a database on this website. Your email address is the reply-to on that message.

The form also sends a hidden field. If that field is filled in, we treat it as spam and do not email it.

Another form endpoint

The project also has a server endpoint at /api/submit for an invitation form. It accepts a legal name, email address, phone number, platform, league name, and team name. If a submission arrives, we email the applicant and email ourselves a copy, again through Resend. We do not save those submissions in a database on this website. The page for that form is not on the site right now. If email delivery is unavailable, the server log can include what was submitted.

How we use information

We use contact details to read your message and reply. We use a website brief only to scope and quote that project. We use payment details only to charge for something you agree to buy and to keep a record of that charge. The host records ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, so the site can run and so we can look into abuse.

Cookies, analytics, and fonts

The pages in this project do not include analytics scripts or advertising scripts. The site code does not set cookies.

The typeface is Montserrat, and the font files are stored on this website. Pages do not load fonts from Google or from another font service.

Payments

If you pay 5280 Content, the payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on this website. Stripe receives the card and billing details needed to complete the charge. Stripe's privacy policy is at https://stripe.com/privacy.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers doing work for us:

  • Resend, to deliver form email. Their policy is at resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Stripe, to process payments. Their policy is at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site. Their policy is at vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice.

Those providers may use the information only to provide the service they were hired for.

How long we keep it

We keep email about a request for as long as we need it to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not keep a separate customer database on this website. Stripe keeps payment records under its own policy. This page does not set a fixed number of days after which everything is deleted.

Asking to see or delete information

Email hello@5280content.com to ask what we have, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will reply to that email. Deleting a message from our inbox does not automatically delete records Stripe holds for a payment.

Children

This site is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, email hello@5280content.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

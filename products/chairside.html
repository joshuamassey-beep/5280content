Chairside

SMS booking and reminders built for stylists and salons.

What it does

Chairside handles the back-and-forth around appointments so stylists don't have to. Clients get automatic reminders before their appointment, can confirm or rebook by replying to a text, and no-shows drop because the follow-up happens without anyone having to remember to send it.

  • Automatic reminders — sent ahead of every booked appointment.
  • Reply-to-rebook — clients can confirm, cancel, or rebook from a text thread.
  • No-show tracking — a running view of who confirms and who doesn't.

Who it's for

Independent stylists, barbers, and small salons who don't want to pay for (or learn) a heavyweight booking platform just to stop losing appointments to forgetfulness.

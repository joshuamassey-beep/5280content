Excel-a-Route

Automated scheduling & dispatch for route-based service businesses.

What it does

Excel-a-Route is a scheduling and dispatch tool that keeps route-based service work moving. It automatically contacts customers by text instead of relying on email or another app nobody opens. Customers get their appointment window, technicians get their route for the day, and everything updates automatically when a job runs long or gets rescheduled.

  • Automated scheduling — appointment windows sent and confirmed by text.
  • Route updates — technicians and customers stay in sync as the day changes.
  • Built for customization — works for any business that runs a route, not just one trade.

Who it's for

Excel-a-Route (working title) is launching on real routes today with clients who service and inspect equipment — evaporative cooler service, energy audits, and fire extinguisher inspections. It's built from day one to work for any service-route business: HVAC, pest control, cleaning, maintenance, and beyond.

TerritoryText

Check outreach territories in and out from the web or by text, logged automatically.

What it does

TerritoryText replaces the paper sign-out sheet for door-to-door outreach teams. Check a territory in or out from a simple web dashboard, or text a territory number for the same result on the go — either way, every check-in and check-out is logged automatically to a shared record, so there's always a clear, up-to-date view of what's out and with whom.

  • Web dashboard — check territories in and out, and see status at a glance, from any device.
  • Text option — no app required, check in or out with a simple text message.
  • Automatic logging — every action is recorded in real time, no spreadsheet needed.

Who it's for

Built for groups that coordinate door-to-door community outreach and need a simple way to track which areas have been covered. Designed to be handed off to any team running a similar territory system — no more shared spreadsheets or paper sign-out sheets.

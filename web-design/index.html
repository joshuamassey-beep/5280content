Questions

What do I get?

The basic package: home / landing page, about us, contact us with a form, services and pricing, an FAQ, a privacy page, a sitemap, basic SEO and AI search setup, local business schema, a mobile-friendly design, SSL and domain connection, contact form spam protection, basic accessibility, and a link to the Google Business Profile. Hosting is $40 a month. Google reviews, book-a-bid scheduling, a map, and the other add-ons are quoted separately. This page does not list prices for those.

How long does it take?

There isn't a published timeline. You see a free demo before you pay, and we tell you timing when we start.

How much does it cost?

$500 one time to build the site, then $40 a month for hosting. Add-ons are quoted separately, and this page does not list prices for them. Hosting continues until you cancel. You can cancel anytime, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You manage hosting in the Stripe customer portal.

Do I need a domain?

The $500 build and the $40 monthly hosting do not include registering a domain name. The basic package does include connecting a domain and SSL. If you already have one, we can use it. If you don't, email us before you pay. A domain price is not listed here.

What does hosting cover?

Hosting is $40 a month and keeps the site we build online. It continues until you cancel.

How does the free demo work?
